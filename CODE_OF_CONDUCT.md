# Code of conduct

TabVerdict welcomes people of every background and experience level.

## Expected behavior

- Be specific, constructive and respectful.
- Critique an idea or implementation, not the person behind it.
- Assume good intent while accepting corrections quickly.
- Protect private data in screenshots, fixtures and reports.
- Make room for people who communicate differently from you.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate disruption and publishing another person's private information are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Serious or repeated incidents can be reported privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile.

This code applies in repository discussions and in public spaces where someone is representing the project.
