01 THE PROBLEM

Twenty-seven tabs.
Three currencies.
Zero confidence.

Store filters optimise for what they sell. Comparison sites optimise for affiliate clicks. Spreadsheets make you copy everything by hand. TabVerdict keeps the useful parts and removes the incentives you cannot trust.

NOBlack-box “AI scores”

A score without a formula is just an opinion in a progress bar.

NOFake “best overall” picks

The best choice changes when your priorities change. So should the winner.

NOSilent data collection

There is no account, analytics SDK, remote database or shopping profile.

02 THREE CLICKS TO CLARITY
01

Press the extension on any product page. TabVerdict reads structured product data and remembers where each value came from.

03 PRIVACY YOU CAN INSPECT

Your shortlist is
not a business model.

TabVerdict requests temporary access only after you click it. The captured board lives in browser storage. There is nowhere for it to upload your data because this repository contains no server.
