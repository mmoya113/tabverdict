TabVerdict is designed so product research can stay on your device. This page explains the boundary without hiding it inside legal fog.

On this page Short versionLocal dataNetwork behaviorExportsDelete dataPermissions
01

The short version

  • No account.
  • No data-collection server.
  • No analytics, ads or affiliate tracking.
  • The page is read only after your explicit capture action.
  • The board is stored on this device.
02

Data processed locally

A capture may read the chosen page's title, URL, image reference, price, currency, brand, availability, rating, review count, structured product data and specification tables. Costs, notes and custom criteria you enter join that normalized product in chrome.storage.local.

03

Network behavior

TabVerdict contains no API endpoint and does not upload your comparison. If a product has a remote image, your browser may request it directly from the source site with a no-referrer policy. The static web demo also has no telemetry and keeps its board in localStorage.

04

Exports

Markdown, CSV, JSON and AI prompts are generated inside your browser. Data leaves TabVerdict only when you choose to copy, download or share it.

05

Delete your data

Use the reset button on the board and confirm the clear action. Removing the extension and its extension data also removes the stored board. TabVerdict uses local storage, not browser sync.

06

Four permissions, four reasons

activeTab

Temporarily read the page you chose.

scripting

Run the packaged extractor on that page.

storage

Retain your board locally.

contextMenus

Offer an optional capture shortcut.

The manifest intentionally has no permanent host_permissions entry.

07

Questions or vulnerabilities

Open a public privacy question in GitHub Issues. Report a vulnerability privately through GitHub Security Advisories.
